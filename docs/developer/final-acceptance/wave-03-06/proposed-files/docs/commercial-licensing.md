# Commercial licensing

SGUI is publicly distributed on npm as commercial software. The package's
[LICENSE](../LICENSE) requires a separate written commercial agreement before
using the library. It grants no automatic free or evaluation license.

The commercial agreement must identify the actual legal licensor and licensee,
and define authorized developers/contractors, permitted applications and
redistribution, price/payment, duration/renewals, support, termination/surviving
rights, governing law, and dispute resolution. These terms are intentionally
not invented in the package notice. Application end-user use is covered only
when the customer's agreement authorizes that distribution and execution.

The license notice is a draft for legal review before publication or customer
use. Counsel should confirm copyright ownership, the publisher's legal identity,
notice acceptance and enforceability, and the agreement's commercial terms.

Third-party dependencies remain under their own licenses. SGUI's commercial
notice does not relicense independently licensed materials, including React,
React Aria, TanStack Table, Lucide and Lexical. THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md retains
current dependency notices, supplemental resolved dependency notices and
historical attributions pending provenance review; dependencies also distribute
their own licenses. Final notice completeness remains a review obligation.

References: [U.S. Copyright Office guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/)
and [MIT license terms](https://opensource.org/license/mit).
