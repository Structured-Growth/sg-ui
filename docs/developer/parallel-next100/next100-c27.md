[F-C27-03](../react-aria-baseline-tasks.md) explicitly assigns insertion selection
preparation, final focus and announcements to the host/editor. The owned
[menu contract](../react-aria-editor-menus.md) and source expose no selection hook.

trigger focus or hand focus to a host-opened dialog; selection preparation,
final editor focus and announcements remain host-owned.” Evidence here covers
that current contract; it does not silently claim an absent wrapper selection API.
